**The problem.** A 5,100-account sales territory was full of duplicates and defunct companies, so outbound effort was aimed at noise instead of real prospects.

**The goal.** Produce a clean, qualified target list that makes outbound efficient.

**What I did.** The decision that mattered was the second cut. After removing duplicates and dead companies took the list from 5,100 to 3,800, I chose a hard qualification criterion -- a confirmed footprint of a specific enterprise platform the product depends on -- over a larger but softer list. That dropped it from 3,800 to 700. I accepted a much smaller target set in exchange for fit, on the bet that fewer right accounts beat more wrong ones.

**The output.** A 700-account qualified target list. On record, the reps working it booked more qualified outbound meetings than any other team in the company.

**Wider application.** Other reps asked for the methodology but found it too tedious to replicate, so it was requested and never adopted. The honest reach is none beyond the originating team -- the method worked but did not spread because it was laborious.
