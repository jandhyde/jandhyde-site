About

When I look at a problem, I'm usually more interested in why it keeps happening than in fixing it this one time. The fix matters, but the system that prevents it from recurring is where the real value is. I'd rather build the thing that fixes the thing.

Most of my career has been spent in the space between departments — where data gets lost, handoffs break down, and nobody owns the process. That space is where the highest-leverage work usually is, and it's where I'm most comfortable.

I build in Salesforce, learn by doing, and default to whatever format makes the decision easier for the person who needs to make it. A useful deliverable on Tuesday beats a polished deck on Friday.

I also built HydeMind — an open-source approach to learning RevOps, curating what you actually need to know and where to start.
